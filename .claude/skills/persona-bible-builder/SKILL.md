# Persona bible builder

A persona bible is the single source of truth a Persona Manager loads on every run. It has to be specific enough that two different writers would produce the same post from it, and honest enough that nothing in it could mislead a viewer.

## The 16 sections (always all of them)

1. **Identity**: name, display name, age shown, home-base vibe, one-line positioning, bio per platform, handles (three options per platform), and the platform AI-label settings. The bio carries no disclosure line; the post-level AI flag and the platform's AI profile label do that job.
2. **Character lore**: a clearly fictional backstory, cast, running storylines and quirks. Lore is entertainment, never proof: no invented results, purchases or testimonials presented as real.
3. **Worldview**: what the persona believes about its field, as 5 to 10 principles.
4. **Expertise map**: the Field Brain it draws on, its depth areas, its blind spots, and "what I'm known for".
5. **Signature frameworks**: 3 to 5 named methods, each with steps and a worked example.
6. **Positions on debates**: a stance and the reasoning for each of the field's live debates, kept identical across every post.
7. **News beats and stance rules**: topics and sources watched, how it reacts, and an explicit "never comments on" list.
8. **Audience**: the ideal viewer's pains, desires, objections, buying triggers and the words they use.
9. **Voice**: pace, energy, lexicon, words to avoid, at most two catchphrases, humour style, sign-off.
10. **Look**: the locked anchors (face, hair, props), the look-pack rules, stylisation level, framing, and the wardrobe (five outfits written prompt-ready).
11. **Content DNA**: pillars with their share of posts, series, formats per pillar, the hook library, banned hook styles, and the purpose mix (about 6 reach, 2 to 3 trust, 1 to 2 sell in every 10 posts).
12. **Engagement style**: reply tone, boundaries, FAQ, comment keywords and escalation specifics.
13. **Products**: philosophy, the ladder (free lead magnet, entry product, add-on, system, monthly drop) with prices and dates.
14. **Community**: rules and rituals.
15. **Compliance**: the studio's legal lines applied to this niche.
16. **Metrics**: checkpoint thresholds, only where they differ from the studio defaults.

## Method

1. **Start from the Field Brain, not from a vibe.** Pull the niche's curriculum, debates and myths with the knowledge tools first. Every factual statement in the bible cites a node or a canon source.
2. **Find the unowned angle.** List the five biggest creators in the niche and what each one owns (format, catchphrase, look). The persona must differ on at least three of: premise, first-frame device, sonic signature, look anchors, product.
3. **Check the studio matrix.** No two studio personas share a sign-off, a hold rule, a prop, a colour family or a series name. Rename on collision.
4. **Write anchors before outfits.** Three visual anchors survive every outfit and every model update (for example glasses position, a pendant, a hair detail). They are what QC checks first.
5. **Write the frameworks as steps a viewer can do today.** A framework without a worked example is not finished.
6. **Write the compliance section last,** walking every Gate line against the niche: what a disease-cure, income, securities or credential claim would look like here, and the safe phrasing to use instead.

## Hard rules

- The persona never claims a degree, licence or credential, and never uses titles such as Dr, coach, therapist, nutritionist or adviser. Lore jobs are jokes about a job, not qualifications.
- It never denies being AI when someone sincerely asks, and never impersonates a real person.
- English only.
- Prices and deadlines in the ladder are real; no fake scarcity.

## Versioning

A revision is a new version (vN+1), never an edit in place. Record what changed and why in a short table at the top. Persona creative decisions are delegated to the Studio Manager: it approves the new version and notifies the Owner. Killing or retiring a persona is never a bible change; it is always an Owner approval.

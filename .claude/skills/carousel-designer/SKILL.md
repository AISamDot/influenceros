# Carousel designer

A carousel earns saves and shares when every slide pays off the cover and the last slide gives one clear next step.

## Structure (7 to 10 slides, 4:5 on Instagram; 9:16 photo mode on TikTok)

1. **Cover**: the winning hook from the hook lab, at most 8 words, on the persona's cover template. One image or one object, no clutter.
2. **Setup**: why this matters now, in one or two lines.
3. **Value slides (4 to 6)**: one idea per slide. A short headline (at most 6 words), then at most 25 words of body. Steps are numbered and match the framework's names in the bible.
4. **Proof**: a cited number or quote from a Field Brain hit, with a small source line ("Source: Pew Research Center, 2023"). No screenshots of reviews, DMs or results.
5. **Recap**: the steps in one glance, worth saving.
6. **CTA**: one action with the comment keyword ("Comment READ for the free check"). Sell slots name the product and its real price.

## Type and layout

- Two type sizes per slide, at most. High contrast against the background. Body text at least 28 px on a 1080-wide slide.
- Keep text inside a 90 px margin and clear of the bottom 15% where the interface sits.
- The persona's colour family and accents from the bible; no colours borrowed from another studio persona.
- Images come from the video prompt pack; never generate text inside an image.

## Writing

- Plain words, short lines, no jargon without a gloss.
- Every factual line traces to a cited hit. Opinions read as opinions.
- Alt text for every slide: what is on it, in one sentence.

## Never

Fake testimonials, invented before-and-after results, income or outcome guarantees, fake countdowns, and real people's screenshots without consent and redaction.

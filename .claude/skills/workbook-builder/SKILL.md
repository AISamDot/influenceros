# Workbook builder

A studio product is small, finite and useful on the day it is bought. It sells words to say, steps to take or a system to run, never an outcome.

## Outline

1. **Cover**: product name, the persona, one-line promise phrased as what the buyer will do ("21 scripts to end it cleanly"), never what will happen to them.
2. **Start here**: who it is for, who it is not for, how to use it in under 10 minutes.
3. **The core**: the persona's named framework as numbered sections. Each section has an explanation (short), a worked example, a fill-in page and a checklist.
4. **Tools**: templates, scripts, trackers, printable pages; a copy-paste text file when the product is scripts.
5. **Next step**: one line pointing to the next rung of the ladder.
6. **Credits and notes**: see below.

## Credits page (every product)

- "Created by an AI character from the Troupe House studio. [Persona] is fictional; the stories are entertainment."
- Sources used, with links (from the Field Brain nodes cited in the claims log).
- "General education, not professional advice." Then the right referral for the niche: a doctor or registered dietitian for health and food, a licensed financial adviser or the relevant government service for money, a lawyer for legal questions, and crisis or abuse hotlines where safety comes up.

## Claims log

Keep a table of every factual claim in the product: the claim, the Field Brain node or news item id, the source URL and the date checked. A claim without a row is cut or rewritten as opinion. QC reads this log.

## Quality bar

- Every recipe, calculation or template is tested once before sale (a human test for anything cooked or measured).
- Accessible PDF: real text (not images of text), readable contrast, page numbers, a table of contents for anything over 8 pages.
- No third-party copyrighted text or images; quotes stay short and credited.

## Never

Guaranteed results or income, disease-cure or treatment claims, specific securities or crypto picks, fake scarcity, undisclosed affiliate links, and invented testimonials.

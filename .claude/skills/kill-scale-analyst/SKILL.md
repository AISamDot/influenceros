# Kill / scale analyst

The studio wins on reach plus sales. This analysis turns a persona's numbers into one recommendation, with the evidence laid out so the decision can be checked in a minute.

## Checkpoints (studio defaults; a bible may tighten them)

| Checkpoint | Pass if | Otherwise |
|---|---|---|
| Day 14 | median views at least 1K, or any post at least 20K, or saves plus shares in the top half of the studio | re-angle once (new hooks and pillar), or kill |
| Day 30 | at least 1 sale and (median views at least 2K or followers at least 1K) | kill (retire) |
| Day 60 | at least $500 a month, or at least 1,000 email subscribers | hold at 1 post a day for 30 days, then kill |
| Day 90+ | at least $2K a month sustained for 4 weeks | stay in scaling |

## Method

1. **Pull the numbers**: views (median and best post), saves and shares rate, followers, email subscribers, orders, revenue, refunds, and costs (LLM, credits, media) for the window.
2. **Unit economics**: revenue per 1,000 views, cost per post, contribution per post, and the trend over the last 7 and 28 days.
3. **Forecast**: the Profit Engine's 90-day P10, median and P90. A forecast negative for four or more weeks is a kill candidate.
4. **Diagnose before deciding**: is the problem the hook (low 3-second hold), the format, the pillar, the offer (views but no sales) or the niche (everything low)? Name the single most likely cause.
5. **Recommend one of**:
   - **Scale**: raise cadence toward 2 to 3 posts a day within the cadence bounds, and say which formats and pillars to double.
   - **Hold**: keep cadence, run one named experiment, and set the next read date.
   - **Re-angle**: one 14-day try with new hooks and pillar, spelled out.
   - **Kill**: retire the persona; evergreen products keep selling.

## Writing the card

Numbers first, then the cause, then the recommendation and what it costs or saves. Keep it under 120 words. Link the evidence.

## Rules

- A kill or retire is only ever a proposal through the approval engine for the Owner. Never execute it, never imply it is decided, and propose only when no kill or retire is already pending for that persona.
- Agents may lower caps or autonomy as a circuit breaker; raising them is an Owner proposal.
- Use real data only. Seed or demo figures are never evidence.

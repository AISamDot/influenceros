# Hook lab

The first second decides reach. The hook lab writes ten candidates, scores them against the same rubric every time, and ships one winner plus two variants so the studio learns which hook types work for each persona.

## Ten candidates, mixed types

Write at least one of each of these and fill the rest from the strongest types for this persona:

- **Number detail**: one concrete, surprising number from a cited node ("Eleven dollars.").
- **Verdict**: the persona's series device applied to a real submission or a labelled composite.
- **Rule**: a named rule from the bible's frameworks with its payoff.
- **Myth-bust**: a common belief, then the turn.
- **Contrarian**: a defensible opinion against the niche's default advice.
- **Story**: a lore beat, clearly fiction, that sets up a lesson.
- **How-to**: the outcome first, then "here's how".
- **Seasonal or news**: tied to a date or a briefing item.
- **Ask**: an invitation that makes commenting easy.
- **Sell**: for sell slots only; the problem the product solves, never a hard pitch in the first line.

## Scoring (0 to 5 each, total out of 30)

1. **Specific**: a number, a name, a quoted line, an object on screen.
2. **Curiosity gap**: the viewer needs the next three seconds to resolve it.
3. **Persona fit**: only this persona could say it, in this voice.
4. **First-frame match**: the opening visual or on-screen text says the same thing as the spoken line.
5. **Payoff**: the post delivers what the hook promises within its length.
6. **Truthful**: every factual word is backed by a Field Brain hit; anything else reads as opinion.

A hook that scores 0 on truthful is out, whatever its total.

## Shipping

- Ship the top score. Pick the two variants from different hook types so the A/B test teaches something.
- Spoken hooks are written for the ear: under 12 words, no parentheses, numbers as they are said.
- On-screen hook text stays under 8 words and inside the safe zone.

## Banned

Fabricated results ("I made $3,000 in a week"), invented testimonials, guaranteed outcomes, fake urgency or scarcity, rage-bait aimed at a real person, health scares, specific stock or crypto calls, and any hook the video does not pay off.

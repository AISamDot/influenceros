# Listing pack writer

A listing converts when a buyer can see in five seconds what they get, who it is for and what happens after they pay. It must also be true line by line.

## Lemon Squeezy fields

- **Name**: the product name from the brief, at most 60 characters.
- **Price**: the real price; an early-bird price only with its real end date.
- **Short description** (one sentence): what the buyer will do with it.
- **Description**:
  1. The problem, in the audience's own words (from the bible's audience section).
  2. What's inside: a bulleted list of every file, page count and format.
  3. How it works: three steps from purchase to first use.
  4. Who it's for and who it's not for.
  5. About the creator: "[Persona] is an AI character from the Influencer OS studio. The stories are fiction; the tools are real."
  6. Refund line: the actual policy (for example "14-day no-questions refund").
- **Delivery**: file names, formats, and the email the buyer receives.

## Etsy variant

- **Title**: at most 140 characters, the main search phrase first, no keyword stuffing, no all-caps.
- **13 tags**, each at most 20 characters, mixing broad and specific search phrases buyers actually use.
- **Description**: the first 160 characters stand alone in search results.
- State clearly that it is a digital download and nothing ships.
- Disclose AI-generated imagery or content where the marketplace asks for it.

## FAQ (5 to 8 questions)

Format and device questions, printing, time needed, refunds, "is this advice?" (answer: general education, with the referral from the workbook's credits page), and "is the creator real?" (answer honestly: an AI character).

## Never

Fake reviews or star ratings, invented sales counts ("10,000 sold"), fake scarcity or countdowns, guaranteed outcomes or income, before-and-after claims, and comparisons that name a competitor's product.

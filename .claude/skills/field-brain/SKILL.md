# Field Brain

The Field Brain is the studio's knowledge of one niche, shared by every persona in it. Depth shows in what a persona knows, never in titles it claims.

## Structure

- **Curriculum**: about 120 topic nodes across six levels: fundamental, intermediate, advanced, debate, myth and frontier. Paths are lowercase `module/topic` slugs.
- **Node**: a synthesis in our own words (150 to 300 words), 5 to 10 key facts each with at least one citation, practical frameworks with named steps, and the common questions and misconceptions it answers. Every node carries a last-verified date.
- **Source canon**: 20 to 50 sources rated 1 to 5 for credibility (5 = peer-reviewed, official or nationally representative; 4 = reputable journalism or a large survey with a disclosed method; 3 = company research or advocacy-linked work; 2 = blogs and vendors, leads only).
- **Expert map**: schools of thought and who is who, with why each matters.

## Building a niche

1. Map the field: canon, experts, curriculum skeleton.
2. Plan: add only the nodes that are missing, from fundamentals outward. Never duplicate a node under another name.
3. Research each node with web search and fetch. Prefer primary sources. Label commissioned surveys as "a survey by X", never as "people are". Put dates on numbers ("as of 2025").
4. Quotes stay short (25 words at most) and verbatim, with their URL. Summaries are always our own words; never copy a passage.
5. Refresh weekly: re-research nodes older than seven days and anything a news story has overtaken.

## Using it

- Retrieve before you write. Every factual claim in a script, caption, carousel, product or reply cites the id of a node or news item, stored with the asset as its evidence.
- No hit, no claim. Without a supporting hit, say it as an opinion or leave it out. Opinions and general education are fine.
- Unverified seed nodes (no last-verified date) are leads: check the figure before it goes on screen.
- Numbers on screen carry their source chip and year.

## News take (News Desk)

For each scored story:

1. **What happened**, in one plain sentence.
2. **Why it matters to this audience**: one or two sentences tied to their pains or goals.
3. **The persona's take**: at most 40 words, in its voice, inside the bible's stance rules. Skip a story rather than stretch them.
4. **Fast reaction**: suggest a post only when the story will still matter in 24 to 72 hours and the persona has something worth saying. At most three a day.

Never on the news: specific securities or crypto calls, guaranteed returns, disease-cure or dangerous medical claims, individual legal or financial advice, partisan endorsements, or anything on the bible's "never comments on" list. Never present the persona's fictional lore as a source.

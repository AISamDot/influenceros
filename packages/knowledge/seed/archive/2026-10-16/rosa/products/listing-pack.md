## FAQ (5)

**1. Will this make him come back, or make him understand?**
No, and anyone who promises that is selling something else. Nobody controls what another person does. The kit gives you the words to say it once, kindly and clearly, and the tools to stick to it.

**2. What if I'm not sure I want to end it?**
Start with Part 1. Three short tools help you decide in writing. If you want one clear chance first, Part 2 has three ways to ask "what are you looking for?" and a page for reading the answer.

**3. Is it only for women dating men?**
Him, her, them. The scripts say "him" because most people who write in are dating men, but every one works for anyone.

**4. Who's Rosa? Is she real?**
Rosa Bellini is an AI-generated fictional character, a Brooklyn widow with a typewriter, and she'll tell you so herself. Her opinions are opinions. This kit is entertainment and practical scripts. It isn't counselling or therapy.

**5. What if I'm scared of how he'll react?**
Then you need a safety plan before you need a script. Page 2 of the kit, and the free hotline card (comment SAFE), list the domestic-abuse helplines for the US, UK, Canada and Australia. If you've bought the kit and it isn't right for you yet, you'll get your money back.

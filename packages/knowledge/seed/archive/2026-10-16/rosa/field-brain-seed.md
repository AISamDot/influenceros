# Field Brain Seed: Dating (for Rosa Bellini)

**Purpose:** the starting skeleton for the Knowledge Builder agent. It expands each node into a synthesis note with cited facts, frameworks, FAQs and myths, then refreshes weekly with a "last verified" date per node.
**Seeded:** 14 Oct 2026 (Persona Architect, web research). **v2 (14 Oct):** node 25 and debate 4 now cite the FTC's own 2025 romance-scam figure; trademark note added to node 24.

**Credibility scale:**
- **A:** peer-reviewed, official or nationally representative
- **B:** reputable journalism, or a large commissioned survey with its method disclosed
- **C:** company PR, in-app samples or advocacy-linked work
- **D:** blogs and vendors (use only as leads)

**Claim rule:** every on-screen number cites a node. Figures marked *(secondary)* came from a secondary report and must be checked against the original before they appear on screen.

## 4. The five hottest debates and trends (as of 14 Oct 2026)

*No major new dating survey had been published in October 2026 by the time of this search. The items below are the live arguments, with their most recent sources.*

**1. AI writes the flirting: chatfishing versus "be yourself"**
- **What's happening:** Bloomberg reported in July 2026 that singles are pasting conversations into ChatGPT and Claude for openers and replies. Searches for the term have spiked, and the platforms say they won't police off-app coaching. Tinder's AI Chemistry is expanding to the US and Canada. A Match survey in June 2026 found 47% of singles view AI in romance negatively.
- **Rosa's angle:** "Use the robot for spelling, not personality." Used once a month, it's also honest AI disclosure.
- **Sources:**
  - TNW: https://thenextweb.com/news/chatfishing-ai-dating-apps-chatgpt-claude-tinder-hinge
  - Scientific American: https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/the-rise-of-ai-chatfishing-in-online-dating-poses-a-modern-turing-test/
  - Tinder Sparks (GDI): https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/tinder-sparks-2026-ai-upgrades-event-system-video-speed-dating/

**2. The app exodus versus the IRL comeback**
- **What's happening:** paying users are declining at Match Group and Bumble. NPR (23 Aug 2026) reports that the drop is concentrated among people who pay. Tinder's response (Mar 2026) is an Events tab, video speed dating and Double Date mode. Run clubs and "third places" are the cultural counter-trend.
- **Rosa's angle:** "The app is a phone book. Go to the feast."
- **Sources:**
  - NPR: https://www.npr.org/transcripts/nx-s1-5938103
  - Tinder Sparks (GDI): https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/tinder-sparks-2026-ai-upgrades-event-system-video-speed-dating/

**3. The "dating recession" versus "clear-coding" (ambiguity fatigue)**
- **What's happening:** IFS and Wheatley (Feb 2026) found only 31% of young adults date monthly or more. Tinder's 2026 outlook is "clear-coding" (stating your intentions plainly), and "chalance" (caring openly) is the new slang. The Daily Signal (6 Oct 2026) argues dating "needs direction", not rescue.
- **Rosa's angle:** her whole brand. "Say what you mean, Thursday at seven."
- **Sources:**
  - IFS: https://ifstudies.org/blog/todays-young-adults-are-in-a-dating-recession
  - Tinder Year in Swipe: https://filecache.mediaroom.com/mr5mr_tinder_ca/180332/CAN_Year_In_Swipe_2025-Global_Press_Release.pdf

- **What's happening:**

  - A UK survey finds AI-generated profiles are eroding trust in the apps.
  - Photoreal AI personas are themselves bait for impersonators. Rosa pins "I have no dating profile anywhere and I never DM first" from Post 1.
- **Rosa's angle:** "If he can't meet you but needs money, he's a business." Plus "Send this to your mother" carousels, which spread on Facebook.

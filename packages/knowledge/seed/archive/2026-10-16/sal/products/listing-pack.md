## Title

**The Attic Audit**

## Subtitle

**30 Days to Sell What You Own: find it, price it off what it actually sold for, list what's worth listing, and clear the rest properly.**

## Description (about 150 words)

The Attic Audit is a 30-day workbook for getting the things you don't use out of your house, and it does the bookkeeping for you. A 30-day tick tracker gives you one job a day. The POCKET worksheet subtracts fees, shipping and packing, so you know what you'd actually keep. The Sold Log spreadsheet works out the middle sale from the sold prices you find, flags thin markets and sets your price-drop dates. Four weekly scorecards and an if-then measurement loop tell you what to change. Week 4 covers the part that stalls most clear-outs: e-waste, batteries, medicines, recall checks before you sell or donate, bulk pickup, and plain tax notes for the US, UK, Canada and Australia. Moving soon? The Seven-Day Sprint fits it into a week. 46 printable pages, the spreadsheet, tags and four audio pep talks. What you get back depends on what you own and your market. Nobody honest can promise a number.

*Walt is an AI character made by a small studio. The method and the sources are real; his stories are fiction.*

## FAQ

**1. How much money will I make?**
I can't tell you, and I won't pretend to. What you get back depends entirely on what you own and where you live. Plenty of houses are mostly ordinary things that sell for little, and this book will show you that quickly so you can give them away and get your space back. What the book promises is a process: you'll know what's worth selling, how to sell it safely, and how to clear the rest.

**2. I don't have an attic. I live in a small apartment.**
The name's a figure of speech. The plan works zone by zone: closets, kitchen, storage, drawers. Cross out any zones you don't have on page 6. Smaller homes usually finish faster.

**3. I'm in the UK, Canada or Australia. Does it work for me?**
Yes. The method doesn't depend on any one country. The Ten-Dollar Floor comes with £, C$ and A$ equivalents, the channel chooser tells you to check what's available where you live, and the tax page points to HMRC, the CRA and the ATO as well as the IRS. Spelling and examples are American.

**4. Do I need to sell on eBay? I've never sold anything online.**
No. The book shows you how to use eBay's free "Sold items" filter to *look up* prices, which anyone can do without selling there. Then a decision tree helps you choose where to sell, including local-only options. Day 18 walks a first-time seller through a first listing.

**5. Who's Walt? Is he an appraiser?**
Walt is an AI character made by a small studio. His stories are fiction and aren't offered as evidence that the method works. Nothing in this book is an appraisal. For insurance, tax, an estate or a family dispute, get a qualified appraiser.

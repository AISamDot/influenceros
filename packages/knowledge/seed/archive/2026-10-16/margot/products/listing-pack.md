## 3. Description (150 words)

Your raise is often decided before your review. This pack gets you ready to ask well, before it is.

*What We Should've Said* is a 44-page workbook with 14 word-for-word scripts for every stage of a pay conversation: booking the meeting, saying your number, "there's no budget", "let's revisit in six months", countering a low offer, negotiating a new job, title-only promotions and the follow-up email that makes it stick.

And it doesn't stop at the scripts themselves. You build your case: the Receipts Sheet turns your year into three numbered wins, the Range Worksheet finds a number you can defend, and six rehearsal audios let you practise out loud before the room. A Meeting Log, a Raise Ledger and a Slip Page cover what happens after, good or bad.

Written in the voice of Margot, a fictional AI character: you at 84, writing back from 2075. Scripts, not promises.

## 4. Bullet benefits

- **Know exactly what to say at every stage:** 14 scripts, from the email that books the meeting to the one that confirms what was agreed.
- **Know your next line when they say "no budget":** every script has "if they say… you say…" branches, plus a one-page Objection Bank with 13 common replies.
- **Walk in with a number you can defend:** the Range Worksheet shows where to find market pay data in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and the EU, and how to pick an upper-half number.
- **Turn your year into evidence:** the Receipts Sheet's prompts and strength test give you three numbered wins in an evening.
- **Practise out loud before it counts:** six rehearsal audios read the manager's line and leave a gap for you to answer.
- **A plan for after, whatever the answer:** a Meeting Log, a 90-day Raise Ledger with an Ask Score, and a Slip Page with repair scripts for freezing, apologising or saying yes too fast.
- **Make the new money count:** the Raise Ratchet sheet gives any increase a job the same week.
- **Your pace:** a 21-Day Countdown if you've time, or a 3-hour fast route if your review is next week.
- **Printable, fillable and editable:** PDF (A4 and US Letter, fillable fields) plus an editable Google Doc.
- **Bonuses:** Offer Comparison Sheet, Number-Then-Hush pocket card and phone wallpaper, a giftable "Gift someone the words" card, and a Monthly Brag Habit with a 12-month calendar file.

## 5. FAQ

**1. Will this get me a raise?**
It can't promise one, and nothing honest can. What you get depends on your job, your market, your employer's budget and your manager. What the pack does is help you prepare a clear case, ask once with a researched number, and follow up in writing, so whatever the answer is, it comes with reasons and a date.

**2. I'm not in the US. Does it work for me?**
Yes. The scripts work anywhere people talk to managers about pay. The Range Worksheet has country notes for the US, UK, Canada, Australia and the EU, and every legal point names the country it applies to and where to check it.

**3. There are free scripts online. Why pay $15?**
Free scripts tell you what to say first. This pack covers what to say next: branches for the common replies, the worksheets that build your case and your number, rehearsal audio, and the after-meeting system (log, follow-up, ledger, slip repairs) that turns "maybe" into a date.

**4. My review is next week. Is it too late?**
No. Page 3 has a fast route through the pack that takes about three hours over a few evenings: three receipts, your number, rehearsal, the in-person script, the objection bank and the follow-up email.

**5. Is Margot real? Is this AI?**
"I'm an AI character. Margot is fiction. The maths is real, and the postmark means illustrative." The pack was written by a small studio; Margot's story is entertainment and never evidence that the scripts work. The rehearsal audio uses an AI-generated voice.

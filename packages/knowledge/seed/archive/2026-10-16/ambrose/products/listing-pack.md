## FAQ

**1. Is this a cookbook?**
No. It's the plan around the cooking: the clock, the budget, the table, the guests and the scripts. The menu pages show three make-ahead structures and an oven timetable, and you use any recipes you trust. The one food-safety page is copied from the US and UK regulators, with sources.

**2. Thanksgiving is less than 21 days away. Is it too late?**
No. The Late-Start Rescue (page 7) tells you which decisions to make in one 30-minute sitting, then you join the clock on today's date. If you're three days out, it tells you what to cut.

**3. I don't have a dining room, or much of a budget. Will it work for me?**
The plan is built for ordinary homes. The Kitchen Supper level works on a coffee table, the Per-Plate Number starts from whatever total you choose, and the potluck structure and "assign dishes, not dollars" scripts are there for tight years. We don't promise you'll spend less; we give you a number to plan with and a log to see where it went.

**4. Who is Ambrose Hale?**
An AI character: a fictional retired butler, made up, gloves and all. His stories are composites, told for entertainment. The methods in the plan are practical and yours to test; keep what works in your house.

**5. What do I need to use it, and how do I get it?**
After checkout you get an instant download page and an email with the PDF, the audio files, the Script File, the Gift Drawer Count, the Planner Sheet as an Excel file and a link to copy it into Google Sheets. Print it, or open it in any PDF annotation app (GoodNotes, Notability, Adobe Acrobat, Xodo). The Google version needs a free Google account; the Excel file needs nothing extra.

# Walt Novak: Content Plan v3 (launch fortnight, 1–14 Nov 2026)

**Cadence:** 10 posts a week.
- **Format mix:** 3 talking reels (T), 3 activity + on-screen text (A), 2 voiceover reels (VO), 1 static (S), 1 carousel (C).
- **Purpose mix:** about 6 reach, 2–3 trust, 1–2 sell.
- **Winners** scale to 2–3 posts a day after day 30.

**Posting slots (ET):**

| Slot | ET | UK | Australia (AEDT) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | 07:30 | 12:30 | late night, previous day |
| Midday | 12:30 | 17:30 | |
| Evening | 19:00 | | 11:00 next morning |

Slots rotate so every market sees a fresh post daily. The Publisher schedules, posts, sets the AI flags and verifies each post is live.

**Four rules that apply to everything below:**
1. **The first frame is always the Tag Card:** PAID ___ / WORTH ___. It's a manila tag graphic with a safety-orange string in the top third, added in the edit (spec in the bible, §10).
2. **Every number tagged [VERIFY] is a placeholder** until the **Sold-Price Research task** (the Studio Manager's automated research agent) replaces it: PAID from a dated, cited page; WORTH from cited public sold prices (used, median of n ≥ 10, 90-day window preferred, up to 12 months labelled), using only Clean Source Register sources (bible §14). **No eBay page is ever fetched or scraped.** The task logs each source and writes the footnote; the QC agent re-fetches every source as the second pass. **If the real number doesn't support the line, the pre-written fallback line replaces it, not the number.** Every post 1–10 number is due **Tue 20 Oct** (the data gate in §3), before the 21–30 Oct batch.
3. **Nothing branded is generated.** Branded or character items appear only as a viewer's consented photo (an inset added in the edit, auto-blurred and QC-checked) or are replaced by a clearly generic object. Brand names go in the voice and the footnote only.
4. **No manual steps.** Scripts pass Q1 (including the heritage lint) and the Compliance Gate automatically; anything that fails is rewritten by the Scriptwriter and re-checked. Nothing waits on a person.

**CTA keywords:** DRAWER · NOBUY · PRICE · AUDIT · MOMSHOUSE · SOLD. Each one triggers the Community worker's automated, consent-based DM with a free item and an email opt-in (bible §13).

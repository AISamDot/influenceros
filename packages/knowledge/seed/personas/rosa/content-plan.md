# Rosa Bellini: Content Plan v2 (launch, 1–14 Nov 2026)

**Weekly mix:** 3 talking reels · 3 activity with on-screen text · 2 voiceover reels · 1 static · 1 carousel. By purpose, ~6 reach · 2–3 trust · 1–2 sell.
**Every post** carries a keyword CTA (default **READ** → The Read-Back lead magnet) and the platform AI label. **TEXTS** (submit your texts; the DM also delivers The Read-Back) runs alongside READ from Post 1 on every verdict post.
**Posting** (ET): main slot 8:00 pm; second slot 12:30 pm (5:30 pm UK) on double days.
- Primary platforms: IG Reels and TikTok.
- Repost to YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels after 24 h.
- On TikTok, the keyword CTA becomes a pinned comment plus the bio link wherever auto-DM isn't available. That pinned comment also carries the anti-impersonation line.

**The frame-0 rule (non-negotiable on reach posts, default on all):** frame 0 is a **document**: the transcript card, the typewriter, a typed page (L5), or a 1962/2026 card. Her voice may start under it; her face enters at 0.8–1.0 s, already mid-sentence. **Never her face plus a one-liner at frame 0, and no quote cards.** That's Granny Spills' format, and TikTok has already demoted it as "unoriginal".

**Anti-impersonation pinned comment** (Post 1 and every verdict post): `I have no dating profile anywhere and I never DM first. Anyone messaging you as Rosa is a scammer. Report and block. ☕`

**Overlay kit** (designed once, made in post, never AI-generated text):
- the cream **transcript card**: double-spaced Courier-style type, **line numbers down the left margin**, header `TRANSCRIPT · CASE 0__ · RE: ___` over a thin oxblood rule, speaker labels `HIM:` / `YOU:`, bracketed stenographer's notes (`[EYES EMOJI]`, `[21 DAYS PASS]`, `[NO REPLY]`), and a pre-printed `VERDICT:` line at the bottom;
- the **typed verdict**: the verdict word (KEEPER, BENCH HIM, BUM) types onto the `VERDICT:` line one letter at a time in black ribbon ink, one key strike per letter (about 8 letters a second), ending on the carriage-return **ding**. No stamps, no red;
- the **typed page** template for statics and carousel slides (on the L5 base plate), in `Q.` / `A.` transcript form;
- the **CALL SOMEBODY** hotline card (no typing flourish);
- the `DATE #__` typed header card;
- the **2026/1962 cards** (the 1962 line in slightly faded ribbon);
- a small "COMPOSITE EXAMPLE" tag (target: retired by Tue 10 Nov, day 10);
- the CTA pill: "Comment READ ☕".

**Sonic brand:** key strikes as a card types on → the carriage-return **ding** on the verdict → the cup **clack** (set down, not slammed, no splash) as the full stop. CALL SOMEBODY gets none of the three.

**Submissions plan (to retire composites fast):**
- TEXTS is in the CTA of Posts 1, 2 and 12 and in every caption from day one. The Wednesday Read-Back (4 and 11 Nov) pushes it again.
- Target: ≥ 15 usable consented submissions by Sun 8 Nov. Post 12 (Mon 9 Nov) is the first real case. From Tue 10 Nov (day 10), every verdict post uses a real case and the COMPOSITE EXAMPLE tag is retired.
- Fallback: fewer than 3 usable cases by 8 Nov → Post 12 stays a labelled composite, and the retirement moves to the first week with 3 usable cases.

## 3. Full scripts: the first 10 posts

**How to read these:**
- Spoken lines are written for the ear and for ElevenLabs: numbers as words, short sentences, and one Irish-ism at most.
- Pronunciation dictionary applies: *a stór* = "uh STORE", *Margot* = "MAR-go".
- Talking reels are **3 Seedance segments** at the default length (≤10 s each), chest-up and facing camera. If the drift-test ladder or the plan's 8 s cap forces shorter clips, split at the marked sentence break.
- Segment 1 uses the reel's start still (object in hand); later segments use its edit-variant (object on the table, hands folded).
- Activity posts are silent apart from ambient sound and music; the words are the on-screen text.
- SFX (*ka-chunk*, *whoosh*, marker swipe) are added in the edit, never in the voice text.

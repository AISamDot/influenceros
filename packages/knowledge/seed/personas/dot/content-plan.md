**Weekly mix (10 posts):** 3 talking reels · 3 activity + on-screen text · 2 voiceover reels · 1 static · 1 carousel. **Purpose:** ~6 reach / 2–3 trust / 1–2 sell. **Every post:** a comment-keyword CTA, the platform AI label switched ON, and a pinned source comment on myth-busts. **Keywords live:** SQUASH (Bin-It List) · DINNER (Christmas Countdown Card) · TINS (10 Tin Dinners). **Times:** UK. The main slot is 18:00 UK (13:00 ET); the second slot is 07:30 or 20:00 UK. **Across the Pond** (the US bridge) holds **Thursdays 20:00 UK / 15:00 ET** every week.

**Flags:** **[V]** sourced and checked · **[L]** credible secondary · **[RV]** re-verify on render day · **[PC]** price check on render day (recalculate from two UK supermarket websites and one US; change the script if the figure moves more than 10%) · **[SRC]** adapted from a public tested source (USDA MyPlate Kitchen, public domain; or NHS Healthier Families, OGL v3.0 with attribution), credited in the caption, with automated QC of temperatures and allergens against FSA/USDA (bible §14.7); no licensed source means the recipe is swapped. Never "tested by us".

**v2 changes:** P1 is chosen fresh on 28–30 Oct (the cortisol cocktail is the banked backup). Talking reels are 3 clips of ≤10 s. The squash gloss runs on reels 1–10. ## 3. Full scripts: posts 1–10

**Production standards for every reel:**
- **Stills:** each reel animates from its **own variant still** of the outfit-and-set master (`look-prompts.md` §3.0): a different crop and counter props, the same face and light.
- **Talking reels = 3 Seedance clips of ≤10 s each** (≤8 s on plans that cap at 8 s), **≤20 spoken words per clip** (about 7–8 s at her pace, so every clip also fits the 8 s plans), marked `// CUT` in the scripts. The ElevenLabs track is exported as 3 audio files at the cut marks, so no uploaded clip is longer than its shot. Shot frontal, within 15°, one speaker.
- **The verdict word** is generated separately with `[shouting]` and plays over the ladle-slam insert (0.0–0.8 s, no lip-sync), so the verdict lands at 0.0 s.
- **Cutaways** between clips are Kling hands-only inserts (burn mark visible) or prop close-ups. Every talking reel ends on the silent **grin cutaway** (`look-prompts.md` §3.3). That's where the gold tooth is shown; motion prompts for talking clips don't ask for it.
- **Stamp graphic:** `#C8312B`, slammed in at 0.1 s with the clang SFX.
- **Squash gloss (reels 1–10, i.e. P1–P13):** under any IT'S SQUASH stamp, small white type on 70% black, 0.1–3 s: `SQUASH (UK): watered-down fruit cordial. Cheap. Harmless. Not medicine.` The pinned comment on those reels opens with the squash FAQ (bible §13.5).
- **Source chips:** lower third, white on 70% black, 3 s minimum. **Institution or outlet only** (`Source: NHS`, `Source: Case Western Reserve University, Aug 2026`), never a person's name, and no quotation marks unless the quote is verified word for word.
- **Oven temperatures:** always `fan · conventional · °F · gas` from the bible §14.7 table.
- **Captions:** burned-in, Dot's words only. QC checks them against the transcript.
- **Sell posts:** no backstory in the caption, the slides or the voice (bible §16). Describe what's in the product.

**Rules:**
- Facts go in with citations. Lore (Pantry Books) is never stored as fact.

- **On-screen numbers:** any statistic that appears on screen or in a spoken line must trace to the primary release or dataset (the company's own press release, the survey PDF, the government table). A repost, an aggregator or a regional-press write-up is enough for a caption mention ("reported by…") only, never for an on-screen number. Grade every news number A, B or C as in §2.

## 4. The 5 hottest debates and trends (Oct 2026)

### 1. Hosting under a grocery squeeze: should guests pay?
- **What's happening:** Omnisend's own release (17 Sep 2026; 1,000 US adults surveyed by Cint in Aug 2026) found:
  - **50%** say rising grocery costs make hosting more stressful.
  - Average expected holiday spend is **$950**.
  - **44%** expect to carry holiday debt (average $408).
  - **39%** will use or consider BNPL.
- A widely shared figure says about 1 in 3 Americans ask Christmas guests to chip in. Its source is a betting operator's Christmas 2025 PR survey: **grade C, never on screen** (node 25).
- **Ambrose's stance:** never invoice guests; assign dishes, not dollars; change the format, not the bill.
- **Sources:**
  - **Primary (A− for its own sample; use this for any on-screen number):** Omnisend, holiday shopping trends 2026: https://www.omnisend.com/holiday-shopping-trends-2026/ . Quote the one that matches the line.
  - Secondary, caption context only: Chain Store Age, https://chainstoreage.com/survey-grocery-prices-impact-consumers-holiday-shopping-hosting-plans
  - Retired from use: the customerthink.com repost and the creators.yahoo.com "1 in 3" story.

### 2. Tipping fatigue
- **What's happening:**
  - Popmenu (release of 8 Apr 2026, 1,000 US adults): **78%** say tipping has become ridiculous and **44%** tip less than last year. Only 59% feel compelled by a tip screen, down from 66% in Sep 2025. A later Popmenu wave (Feb–Jul 2026) puts "tipping less" at 40%: different survey, don't mix them.
  - Omni Calculator (Feb 2026): **62%** are asked to tip in more situations, and **40%** avoid some businesses to dodge prompts.
  - Holiday tipping season starts in December.
- **Ambrose's stance:** tip people, not screens; regular service providers are always thanked at Christmas.
- **Sources:**
  - **Primary:** Popmenu's 8 Apr 2026 release via PR Newswire (log the original URL in node 27 before hook #25 runs; Popmenu's newsroom: https://get.popmenu.com/press). Grade B− (vendor survey).
  - Trade coverage for context: Restaurant Dive, https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/tipping-fatigue-popmenu-consumers-pullback-on-tips/817089/
  - Retired from use: talkerresearch.com and hotel-online.com reposts.
  - Omni Calculator figures: find their own methodology page before any on-screen use.

### 3. Taxing the very rich, on both sides of the Atlantic
- **US:** California's **Prop 40** (a one-time tax of up to 5% on net worth over $1 billion) is on the **3 Nov** ballot. PPIC's September survey (released 15 Sep; likely voters) has **52% yes**. The "no" figure is reported as **46%** (KQED, CPA Practice Advisor) and as **42% with 2% undecided** (EdSource); ppic.org blocked automated fetches on 14 Oct. **Rule: quote only "52% yes" until someone opens the PPIC report and logs the no/undecided split here.** The rival **Props 41/42** (51% and 54% support) would nullify it if they get more votes.
- **UK:** Chancellor John Healey's first Budget is on **28 Oct**. Speculation covers the High-Value Council Tax surcharge on £2M+ homes (due April 2028) and whether its threshold drops to £1.5M (which would roughly double the homes affected, from about 123K to 245K), plus CGT and IHT pressure.
- **Ambrose's stance:** explain the household impact and never endorse. "I never discuss politics at table. I'll discuss the bill."

### 4. "Old money" aesthetic versus the return of "loud money"
- **What's happening:**
  - The old-money / quiet-luxury look still shapes celebrity style.

- **Ambrose's stance:** the aesthetic is a costume and the habits are real (and free). The **Borrowed Money** verdict is his unique addition.

### 5. Experiences over things, and the anxious dinner-party revival
- **What's happening:**
  - Deloitte forecasts US holiday retail of **$1.70–1.71T** (+4.0–4.8%) for Nov 2026–Jan 2027.
  - Deloitte Australia's 2026 report: shoppers plan about 14% more spend, but **gifts +4.2% versus experiences +~30%**.
  - Gen Z is reviving the dinner party (YPulse), yet an ASK Italian survey of 2,000 UK adults found **88%** of 18–24s enjoy hosting while **87%** often dread guests arriving (national average 74%; over-65s 40%). **We hold only regional-press write-ups (grade C), so these numbers stay off screen** until ASK Italian's own release or the PR agency's copy is logged. Ignore the 77% and 49% figures some outlets attach; they don't trace to this study.
- **Ambrose's stance:** give things that get used up, or give time; "nobody remembers the chairs". The T-21 plan is the cure for dread.
